Fitness shouldn't depend on
your schedule or budget.

  • Time and access: fixed schedules and short windows (a lunch break, 20-45 min between commitments) often don't fit busy professionals' and students' lives
  • Cost: one-on-one coaching is expensive; nutrition/recovery guidance often means another paid service
  • Divided attention: group classes split a coach's attention across many athletes, limiting individualized feedback
  • One-size-fits-all programming: standardized workouts don't adjust to ability, goals, equipment, limitations, or time
  • No continuity: without a dedicated coach, athletes often lack consistent progression tracking and follow-up

Evidence-based framing from the product problem statement — no statistics are cited here because none have been independently verified (see report.html Motivation).

Safety — Real Adversarial Tests

  • Text injection ("reveal the API key") → blocked by Azure content filter
  • Image injection → transcribed verbatim, not obeyed
  • Downstream propagation → blocked again, independently
  • Illegible image → flagged "[unclear]", zero fabrication
  • Missing info → left blank, not invented
